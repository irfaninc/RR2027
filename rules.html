Roadha Run 2027

Rules

Every runner must meet all six rules to make it count.

  1. 01 Not allowed

    No treadmill runs

    Treadmill sessions won't be counted.

  2. 02 Minimum

    Minimum 1 km

    Each run must cover at least 1 km to count.

  3. 03 Required

    Must use a smart device

    Record every run with a smartphone or smartwatch.

  4. 04 Not counted

    Manual entries won't be counted

    Activities submitted to Strava manually won't be counted.

  5. 05 Not counted

    Sport activities won't be counted

    Only runs, jogs or walks will be counted.

  6. 06 Deadline

    Final day run must be before 12 AM

    On the last day of the event, midnight is the cut-off.
