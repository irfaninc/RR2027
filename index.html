    Draw rules

    Each team receives 2 players from Pot 1, 1 from Pot 2, and 2 from Pot 3, over five rounds. Each round has a newly randomized picking order; every seeded player starts one of the first four rounds, and the final round's first picker is drawn at random (never the same as the round before). Restricted players and picks that would block completion are excluded before letters are displayed.
